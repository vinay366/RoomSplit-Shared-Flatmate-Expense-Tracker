How it works

From first tap to fair settlement

Four steps. No spreadsheets. No WhatsApp arguments.

1
Create your room
Enter your phone number, get an OTP, pick a room name. RoomSplit generates a 6-character invite code — share it on WhatsApp and your flatmates join in 30 seconds.
2
Log what you bought
Tap +, pick a category (Vegetables, Milk, Gas, Rent, Misc), type the item and amount. Done in under 10 seconds. The entry lands on everyone's feed instantly.
3
Watch the balance update
Every entry auto-updates the Balance tab. It shows each person's net — how much they've overpaid or underpaid versus the group average — recalculated in real time.
4
Settle up at month-end
The app reduces all debts to the minimum number of transfers. Tap "Settle" to copy a UPI link, pay in seconds, and start the next month at ₹0 for everyone.

Features

Everything your flat needs, nothing it doesn't

Built for the real daily rhythm of shared living — not for corporate expense reports.

📋
Shared live feed
Every entry lands on a shared timeline visible to all members, in real time. No more "did you add it?" messages.
⚖️
Auto balance
Balances recalculate on every entry. No manual splitting, no formulas. Just a clear number per person, always accurate.
🔔
Instant notifications
When Ravi buys vegetables, everyone gets a push notification. The group stays in sync without a single WhatsApp message.
📊
Monthly summary
Total spend, per-person share, and a category breakdown chart — so you know exactly where the month's money went.
🔗
UPI settle-up
Tap Settle to copy a UPI deep link. Open any UPI app, paste it, and the payment goes through in seconds. No re-typing amounts.
🔒
Phone auth, no password
Join with your mobile number and an OTP. No email, no password to forget. Your session stays active for 30 days.
Balance view

Crystal-clear at month-end

RoomSplit calculates who's paid more or less than their fair share, then tells you the minimum transfers to settle everything — no back-and-forth needed.

✓
Equal split, no configuration
Assumes equal share for all expense types. Simple for flatmates who share costs evenly.
✓
Debt simplification
Instead of everyone paying everyone, the app reduces settlements to the fewest possible transfers.
✓
Per-month history
Switch months to review any prior period. Your data stays as long as the room is active.
